The **Mila World Modelling Reading Group** meets weekly at [Mila](https://mila.quebec/) to discuss
recent work on world models: learned simulators of environments, and the video models, agents
and planners built on top of them.

Each week one participant presents a paper and the rest of the hour is open discussion. Despite
the name, there is no reading requirement — come along even if you have not read the paper.
The group is open to everyone at Mila, and to visitors.
